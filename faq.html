FAQ - Maintenance And Troubleshooting

Basic rules and quick checks for students and lab users working with the Phygital Lab models.

Maintenance Of The Lab

Daily Lab Care

How should the lab table and room be maintained?
  • Regularly clean the table using a vacuum cleaner to prevent dust accumulation.
  • Maintain cleanliness in the lab room.
  • Avoid dirty shoes and construction dust near the model area.
  • Use a vacuum cleaner for regular cleaning.
Which water levels should be monitored?
  • Sewage Treatment tank.
  • Data Center coolant tank.
What actions should be avoided?
  • Disconnecting or connecting wires without consulting the team and documenting changes on the wiki.
  • Plugging incompatible hardware into the PoE switch.
  • Changing the IP address of any hardware without documentation.
  • Eating or drinking on the table.
  • Physically moving buildings on the model.
  • Operating moving parts, such as metro or turbines, at high speed or stopping them by force.
  • Sharing credentials with users or students, to prevent unauthorized resetting or reprogramming.
What should be done before hardware or software changes?

Consult the responsible team first, then document the change details on the wiki. Include date, device name, IP address if applicable, reason for change, and rollback notes.

Basic Troubleshooting

When A Scenario Stops

Dashboard access issues

Confirm that the system is connected to the lab network. Refer to the lab network connection step or network documentation before checking the dashboard URL again.

Non-responsive scenarios
  • Check the device IP address.
  • Check the physical power status in the panel.
  • Ping the IP address using command-line tools to confirm connectivity.
Specific hardware issues
  • Toll Plaza Arduino Freeze: Use the reset buttons near the toll plaza. It takes around 30 seconds to reboot.
  • Raspberry Pi Malfunction: Reboot by toggling the MCB in the corresponding panel.
  • Sewage Treatment Pumps: Ensure the water level is above 20%.
  • Data Center Coolant Pumps: Check that the water level is above the lower sensor.
  • Sorting Belt Pushers: Inspect for obstructions in the servo and pusher mechanisms.
  • Fire Sensors Triggering: Cover the sensors or disable them in the Node-RED flow.
How do I ping a device?

Use a command-line tool and run ping <device-ip>. If replies are received, the device is reachable. If not, check power, network cable, IP address, and panel status.

Additional Note: For unresolved issues, contact the Cyberange Team for assistance.
