Lab Guide

FAQ - Maintenance And Troubleshooting

Basic rules and quick checks for students and lab users working with the Phygital Lab models.

Maintenance

Daily Lab Care

Can models or buildings be moved?

No. Do not move model structures, tracks, tanks, racks, buildings, vehicles, or wiring without approval from the responsible team.

Can we connect new devices to switches or controllers?

No. Unknown hardware must not be connected to PoE switches, controllers, Raspberry Pi boards, PLC panels, or lab networks without approval.

Can credentials be shared with students?

No. Dashboard, server, controller, Wi-Fi, and admin credentials must not be shared casually. Use approved student accounts or demo accounts.

How should the lab be cleaned?

Use gentle dry cleaning or a vacuum around the table. Keep food, drinks, dust, and loose objects away from electronics and moving parts.

Troubleshooting

When A Scenario Stops

Dashboard is not opening. What should I check?

Confirm the system is on the lab network, check server power, verify the IP address, and test reachability using ping <device-ip>.

A model is not responding. What next?

Check power, MCB status, network cable, controller status, and whether the correct scenario flow is running. Record the issue in the wiki/log notes.

Moving parts are stuck or running too fast. What should I do?

Stop the scenario from the control dashboard if available. Do not physically force train, barrier, conveyor, turbine, or gate movement.

Before changing hardware or software, what is required?

Consult the responsible team and update the wiki with date, change details, device names, IP addresses, and rollback notes.
